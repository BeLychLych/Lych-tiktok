Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Summary

LYCH Video Uploader is a personal desktop tool. It only uses the TikTok data it needs to upload videos to the connected account as drafts. Nothing is sold or shared, and everything stays on the owner's own computer.

2. What the app receives from TikTok

  • Basic profile info (scope user.info.basic): your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar. This is only used to confirm which account is connected.
  • Access tokens: issued by TikTok when you approve the app. They let the app upload videos as drafts (scope video.upload).

The app never receives your TikTok password. It doesn't read your messages, followers, likes, comments or viewing history.

3. How the data is used

The data above is only used to upload videos you choose to your own TikTok account as drafts. It isn't used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Where it is stored

Tokens and basic profile info are stored in a local file on the owner's computer. The app has no server or database, and doesn't send this data anywhere except to TikTok's official API.

5. Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.

6. Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept only while the app is connected to your account. To delete it, remove the app's access in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services; this stops the tokens from working right away. The local token file can also be deleted at any time.

7. Children

The app is not meant for anyone under 18 and doesn't knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
