LYCH Video Uploader

A personal tool for posting Roblox avatar outfit videos

Terms of ServicePrivacy Policy

LYCH Video Uploader is a private desktop tool used by one creator. It uploads the creator's own Roblox avatar outfit videos to the creator's own TikTok account as drafts. The creator then reviews each draft in the TikTok app, adds a sound and posts it.

The tool is not offered to the public and does not post to anyone else's account.
